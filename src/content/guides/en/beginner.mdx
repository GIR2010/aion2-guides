Everything worth knowing in your first hours, so you don’t have to dig through forums. Setting and menu names match the English 2.0.7 client word for word, so you can find them with the search box in the settings window. Tips from third-party guides that could not be checked against the client are marked.

## The game in one minute

- **The world is Atreia**, split into two halves. You play a **Daeva**, an immortal warrior of one of two factions: **Elyos** or **Asmodians**. The factions are at war, and players of the other faction are enemies.
- **Flight** is part of the game, not a bonus: you get wings on the first Ascension quest (around level 5), and from then on you fly across the world and fight in the air.
- **The Abyss** is territory shared by both factions, where PvE mixes with PvP. It opens at 45.
- **The cap is level 45.** The story (Episode) takes you there; after that comes seasonal content: dungeons, raids, the Abyss, the arena. Seasons change the dungeon and reward lists.

## First decisions

### Faction and server

**You can’t change your faction or server** (per third-party guides, character transfers aren’t available yet). Agree with friends beforehand: your faction decides your starting zones, allies and side in PvP. All 8 classes are available to both factions.

### Class

| Class | Weapon | Party role | For beginners* |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gladiator | Greatsword | Tank or damage | Easy |
| Templar | Sword and shield | Tank | Easy |
| Ranger | Bow | Ranged damage | Easy |
| Assassin | Daggers | Melee damage | Hard: you need to stay behind the target |
| Spiritmaster | Orb | Caster, ranged | Medium |
| Sorcerer | Spellbook | Caster, ranged | Hard |
| Cleric | Mace | Healer | Medium |
| Chanter | Staff | Healer or damage | Medium |

\* Difficulty ratings come from third-party guides, not the client. Roles come from the client class table (`PcData`). Assassin leveling guide: [Assassin 1→45](../assassin-leveling/).

### Control mode

| Mode | How it plays | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| **AION 2** (action) | Crosshair in the screen centre, the camera follows the mouse without clicking, targets are picked near the crosshair. The cursor shows only while you hold the cursor key or a window is open | Players who like action combat and direct aiming |
| **AION 1** (classic) | Cursor always visible, camera rotates with a mouse button held, targets by click, click-to-move. Mouse buttons can’t be bound to skills or macros | Players used to tab-targeting |

You can switch at any time: **Settings → Combat → Controls → Control Mode**. Try both in the first zones and stick with one: your key layout is built around it.

## Settings to change right away

### See the boss’s back and front

| Where | Setting | What to pick |
|---|---|---|
| Info Display → Combat | **Display Boss Orientation Indicator** | **ALL** — the boss’s front and back zones are highlighted on the ground. Melee classes, especially Assassins, should use at least **BACK** |
| Info Display → Combat | **Aggro Range Display** | On — shows how close you can get before the targeted monster attacks on its own. Transparency is adjustable |
| Info Display → Combat | **Display Skill Range** | On — shows where your skill will land |

The indicator works on bosses only. For regular monsters, read the back from the model; back attacks hit harder for every class, and the Assassin’s whole damage is built on them.

### Cut visual noise on bosses and in crowds

| Where | Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Graphics → Optimize | **Player Particle Effect Display** | **Self and Party** — other players’ skill effects disappear. Solo you can use **Self Only** |
| Graphics → Optimize | **Display Identical Player Skins** | On in crowds and at mass bosses: other characters (except party and force) look the same, lower load |
| Graphics → Optimize | **Mass Combat Quality** | Lower it if FPS drops in crowds. Too low and enemies appear late |
| Info Display → Common | **Display Other Players’ Pets** | Off |
| Info Display → Common | **Display Player Nameplate** | Off, or **Display as Tier Icon** — party names are always shown |
| Graphics → Camera / Direction | **Display Equipped Title Effects**, **Display Equipped Title Effects on Nameplate** | Off, or the “self only” option |
| Graphics → Camera / Direction | **Camera Shake Effect During Combat**, **Hit Freeze Effect** | Off if they make the fight hard to follow |
| Graphics | **Teleport Effect Color** | **Dark** — the loading screen doesn’t flash white |

### Controls and targeting

| Where | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Combat → Controls | **Skill Queue** | On: a skill pressed during another one fires next instead of being lost |
| Combat → Target Scanning | **Fair Play Mode** | On while leveling: auto-targeting skips monsters other players attacked first |
| Combat → Target Scanning | **Preferred Search Direction** | **Front of Camera** — changing target picks what you see |
| Graphics → Camera / Direction | **Camera Lock-On to Target** | Try it on bosses; turn it off if it gets in the way of circling the target |
| Graphics → Camera / Direction | **Maximum View Distance** | **Far** — you see more on bosses |

Skill macros (one key, a chain of skills) are set up in the skill window; how they work is covered under “Combat” below.

### Performance

- Start from **Quick Settings**, then adjust one option at a time.
- Lower **shadows**, **reflections**, **effects** and **post-processing** first: they cost the most and matter least for visibility.
- Keep the **Frame Generator** off if reaction time matters: it adds latency. Turn **NVIDIA Reflex** on if available.
- An **Upscaler** (DLSS or FSR) on “Quality” raises FPS with little loss of sharpness.
- Check FPS in a town and in crowds, not in an empty zone.

### Interface

- **Edit HUD** (available from the start): move, resize, group and hide elements. Save the layout to one of 6 presets; you can export it as a **code** or a **file** and load it on another character.
- **Potion auto-use**: drag a potion into the auto-use slot and set the HP threshold in the settings (**Combat → Controls → Life Potion**).
- **Combat Analysis** is available from the start: handy for comparing skill layouts and macros.
- **Buffs and scrolls don’t fire on their own** — put them into auto-use too. Food and scrolls last a few minutes (5 per third-party guides), so use them before the boss, not at the dungeon entrance.

## Combat

- **Basic attacks** restore MP; **heavy attacks and skills** spend it. Hold the attack key and the combo repeats without separate presses.
- **Dodge** has its own key. Dangerous monster and boss attacks are marked on the ground — step out of the area.
- **Back attacks** are noticeably stronger. Turn on the boss orientation indicator (see settings).
- **Stagger.** Bosses have a stagger gauge. Skills whose description lists stagger damage lower it; at zero the boss is briefly defenceless. That’s the best window for your strongest skills, and some skills only work on a staggered target.
- **Macros.** Several skills are stacked in a column and run while you hold the key. Priority goes bottom to top: the macro uses the lowest available skill and skips those on cooldown. A skill can sit in only one macro. In AION 1 mode a macro can’t be triggered with mouse buttons.
- **Skill specializations must be equipped by hand.** Unlocking a specialization slot isn’t enough: drag a specialization into the slot in the skill window, or the bonus does nothing.

## How your character grows

| System | From level | What matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Skills** | 4 | Skill Points come from levels and from Wisdom Stones in Sealed Dungeons. Raise the skills you press all the time first |
| **Ascension** | 5, 22, 32, 45 | Ascension quests open after the Episode and a full Ascension gauge. The first gives wings, the second unlocks stigma |
| **Titles** | 7 | From achievements. Up to **3** can be equipped at once, and they give stats |
| **Enhancement** | 9 | Enhancement stones upgrade gear. Early gear is replaced quickly — don’t spend stones on it before 45 |
| **Item Extraction** | 10 | Extract gear you don’t need instead of deleting it: it gives enhancement materials and skins for the Closet |
| **Daevanion** | 12 | Boards bought with Daevanion Crystals: +1 to skill levels and stats. Spend crystals right away |
| **Stigma** | 22 | Special class skills. Slots open at 22/27/32/37, levels cost Stigma Shards |

**Item Level and Might are different numbers.** Item Level counts only equipped gear. Might also includes Daevanion, Pantheon, Arcana, skill levels, stigma, titles and wings — groups recruit for hard content by it.

**Skill reset** is done in the skill window; per third-party guides it’s free up to level 31 and costs Kinah after that. Experiment with your layout while it costs nothing.

## The road to 45

Almost all XP comes from the **yellow Episode quests**. Quests alone aren’t enough, though: clear the rest along the way, or you’ll reach 45 short on gear, crystals and Skill Points.

| Level | What opens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Wings, flight | First Ascension |
| 10 | Gathering, crafting, Item Extraction, Substance Morph, Daeva Pass, Navigator, daily rewards | Gather resources as you go — Essence Extraction improves with use |
| 12 | **Regional Quests** | Blue quests are accepted automatically when you’re nearby. They give Daevanion Points and gear and are needed for the Ascension gauge |
| 13 | **Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds** | “?” icons on the map. Dungeons are short and give Wisdom Stones and Daevanion Crystals, Strongholds give treasure. Progress per region: **Map → Exploration** |
| 16 | **Monoliths**, Legion | Collect Empyrean Traces — resonance gives rewards and an amulet enhancement scroll. Join a Legion: bonuses and people for group dungeons |
| 22 | Stigma, Expeditions (Krao Cave), Pantheon | The “Exploration” difficulty can be done solo |
| 25 | Supply Requests | Hand in items for rewards |
| 30 | **Daily dungeon** Unknown Fissure | Up to 14 times a week, gives enhancement stones |
| 30 / 37 | Expeditions Urugugu Canyon, Fire Temple | Also have an “Exploration” difficulty |
| 45 | Abyss, Transcendence, Sanctuary, Arena, Nightmare | See below |

A detailed route and layout are in the [Assassin guide](../assassin-leveling/) (XP per level and dungeons there are calculated from client tables).

**Shugo Festival** (mini-games) opens early, but per third-party guides its rewards scale with your level and keys recharge once a day. It pays to spend them at 45.

## Getting around

- **Flight** uses FP; when it runs out, you fall. Watch the gauge over chasms.
- **Flight Circles** restore FP. **Windstreams** are paths glowing blue: jump at the start or fly into one and it carries you forward. An **Updraft** launches you upward if you approach it on foot; then jump to glide.
- **Kibelisks** are teleport points: bind them and travel through the map.
- **Auto-move** to a quest objective is a button in the quest list. You can travel that way while sorting your inventory.
- **Leaving a dungeon** — the button at the top left of the minimap. The arrow next to a quest in the notifications teleports you to its location.

## After 45 in brief

| Content | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Abyss** | Territory of both factions: PvE and PvP, Abyss Points for monsters and quests. Worth it even without PvP |
| **Expeditions on “Conquest”** | The hard mode of group dungeons, requires Item Level |
| **Transcendence** | Seasonal 5-player dungeon with stages; the main source of Arcana |
| **Sanctuary** | Weekly 10-player raid |
| **Nightmare**, **Ascension Trial** | Solo seasonal challenges |
| **Arena and Battlefield** | 1v1, teams, 10v10 |
| **Spacetime Rift** | A portal to the enemy faction’s continent at random times |
| **Duty Quests** | Simple daily quests |

## Beginner mistakes

- **Picking a faction or server at random** — you can’t change it later.
- **Grinding monsters instead of Episode quests.**
- **Skipping Regional Quests, Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds** — they’re what gives Skill Points, crystals and gear.
- **Enhancing gear before 45** — it will be replaced anyway. If you’re stuck, raise several items by 1 stage rather than one item by many: the failure chance grows with the stage.
- **Not equipping skill specializations** after unlocking a slot.
- **Hoarding Daevanion Crystals** — spend them right away.
- **Turning on Auto Extract without checking the filters** — an extracted item can’t be recovered.
- **Using food and scrolls at the entrance** instead of before the boss.

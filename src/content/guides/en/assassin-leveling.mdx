For players who just started an Asmodian Assassin and want to reach 45 quickly and cleanly. Level-cap progression has its own guide.

## How leveling works

- **XP.** Yellow (story) quests give almost all the XP to 45. An experienced player gets there in 12–15 hours, a new player in 20–25. Grinding mobs is not worth it.
- **The slow part is 29–39.** The story covers only about 70% of the XP needed here. Fill the gap with **Sealed Dungeons**: each gives Skill Points and Daevanion Points.
- **Skill Points** start at level 4. They raise a skill up to level 10; above that, skill levels come from Daevanion boards and gear.
- **Daevanion boards** open like this: Nezekan at 12, Zikel at 20, Vaizel at 30, Triniel at 40, Azphel at 45.
- **Stigma** unlocks at 22 after the 3rd Ascension quest. Slots: 22 / 27 / 32 / 37. Raised with Shards: 1 per level from 23 to 39, 2 per level from 40.

## Where to spend Skill Points

Points granted per level and the cost of each skill level are still being verified in game. Until then, follow this order:

| # | Skill | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quick Slice | Hits up to 4 targets — main pack damage while leveling |
| 2 | Heart Gore | Triggers after a crit, restores MP. Later boosted by the Illusive Clone stigma |
| 3 | Insignia Explosion (from 14) | Detonates Insignias — the main damage source against tough targets |
| 4 | Savage Roar | Stacks Insignias for Insignia Explosion |
| 5 | Shadowstrike | Opener: blink behind and stun |

**Passives** in priority order: Rear Smite → Assault Stance → Exploit Weakness → Defense Break → Determination.
Skip Apply Poison and Ambush Stance in PvE.

## Stigma while leveling

| Slot (level) | Stigma | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (22) | Illusive Clone | Removes the Heart Gore cooldown and gives +20% damage for 10s |
| 2 (27) | Savage Fang | Heavy hit plus 5 Insignias at once for Insignia Explosion |
| 3 (32) | Swift Contract | +20% Combat Speed |
| 4 (37) | Triniel's Dagger | Hardest single hit |

You collect about 29 Shards by 45, while one stigma needs 75 to reach level 20. So don't spread Shards thin while leveling:
first take **Illusive Clone to 5** (specialization unlocks at 5), then **Swift Contract to 5**, and put the rest into Illusive Clone.

## Solo leveling rotation

**Single target (elite, quest boss):**
Shadowstrike → Ambush (+30% from behind) → Savage Roar ×N → Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore after a crit.
From 22, pop Illusive Clone before the fight so you can spam Heart Gore.

**Mob pack:**
pull 2–3 mobs → Quick Slice (up to 4 targets) between Savage Roars → Insignia Explosion on the tankiest target.

**When things get dangerous:** Flash Slice (3s Blind), Defiance (breaks crowd control).

## Common mistakes

- Attacking from the front. Almost all Assassin damage is built around back attacks.
- Grinding instead of questing. Story quests give far more XP.
- Skipping Sealed Dungeons at 29–39.
- Putting points into Apply Poison and Ambush Stance.

For players who run an Asmodian Assassin and want to reach 45 quickly and cleanly. Skill values and descriptions come from the global client (screenshots taken 2026-10-19). English skill names are community names; the in-game Russian names are on the Russian page. Level-cap progression has its own guide.

## How leveling works

- **XP.** Yellow (story) quests give almost all the XP to 45. An experienced player gets there in 12–15 hours, a new player in 20–25. Grinding mobs is not worth it.
- **The slow part is 29–39.** The story covers only about 70% of the XP needed here. Fill the gap with **Sealed Dungeons**: each gives Skill Points and Daevanion Points.
- **Skill Points** start at level 4. They raise a skill up to level 10; above that, levels come from gear and Daevanion boards.
- **Skill level is capped by character level.** Skill level L becomes available at character level `skill unlock level + 3 × (L − 2)`, i.e. +1 skill level every 3 character levels. Try earlier and the game says “Can be learned at character level N”.
- **The first specialization unlocks at skill level 8** (pick one of three), with one more each at 12 and 16. “+N skill” bonuses from gear count too.
- **Daevanion boards:** Nezekan at 12, Zikel at 20, Vaizel at 30, Triniel at 40, Azphel at 45.
- **Stigma** unlocks at 22 after the 3rd Ascension quest. Slots: 22 / 27 / 32 / 37. Learning a stigma costs 1 Shard; specializations unlock at stigma levels 5 / 10 / 15 / 20.

## Skill Points: what to raise and when

While leveling you have enough points for about 9–10 of the 22 skills. Don't spread them thin.

| # | Skill | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quick Slice | Up to 4 targets, no cooldown, restores 120 MP — the core of pack damage |
| 2 | Savage Roar | Up to 4 targets + 1 Insignia each, +20% skill speed |
| 3 | Heart Gore | After a crit: up to 4 targets, +100 MP. No cooldown under Illusive Clone |
| 4 | Insignia Explosion (from 14) | Main damage: up to 4 targets, stun. At 5 Insignias the stun is guaranteed vs NPCs |
| 5 | Ambush | Heavy hit, +30% and 2 Insignias from behind |
| 6 | Shadowstrike | Opener: 20 m blink behind the target and a 3s stun |
| 7 | Exploit Weakness | +crit (feeds Heart Gore), clones engrave Insignias |
| 8 | Rear Smite | +back attack damage and +5% PvE damage |
| 9 | Assault Stance | +crit damage |
| 10 | Defense Break | −20% target Defense after a stun — up almost all the time |

Other passives (Heightened Sixth Sense, Impact Hit, Ambush Stance) get 1–3 points on cheap levels if anything is left. **Apply Poison** is PvP-only.

## Specializations at skill level 8

| Skill | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Slice | Multi-hit chance +50% | Pack damage. If you die a lot, take “Absorbs 0.7% HP” |
| Savage Roar | Skill speed +20% | Faster animations, faster Insignias |
| Shadowstrike | Crit damage +20% for 10s | Boosts the whole burst after the opener |
| Ambush | 2 Insignias on hit | Insignias even when not behind |
| Heart Gore | 1 Insignia on hit | Every proc feeds Insignia Explosion |
| Insignia Explosion | Multi-hit chance +50% | More damage on all 4 targets |

## Stigma while leveling

| Slot (level) | Stigma | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (22) | Savage Fang | Up to 4 targets and 5 Insignias at once. Follow with Insignia Explosion — the whole pack is stunned |
| 2 (27) | Illusive Clone | 10s with no Heart Gore cooldown + damage equal to 20% of Attack |
| 3 (32) | Swift Contract | +20% combat speed for 10s |
| 4 (37) | Assault Ambush | Up to 4 targets, guaranteed Blind vs NPCs. For bosses and at cap — Triniel's Dagger |

Shards are scarce while leveling: about 29 by 45. Don't spread them:
first **Savage Fang to 5** (first specialization), then **Illusive Clone to 5**, then **Swift Contract to 5**. Put the rest into Savage Fang.

## Solo leveling rotation

**Mob pack (from 22):**
Shadowstrike on the center target → **Savage Fang** (5 Insignias on 4 targets) → **Insignia Explosion** (100% stun vs NPCs, triggers Defense Break) → Savage Roar and Quick Slice → Heart Gore after every crit.

**Elite or quest boss:**
Illusive Clone → Shadowstrike (behind, stun) → Ambush (+30% from behind) → Shadow Fall (knockdown on the stunned target) → Savage Roar ×N → Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore as often as the clone allows.

**Before 22:** the same, but stack Insignias with Savage Roar and Ambush.

**When things get dangerous:** Flash Slice (guaranteed Blind vs NPCs), Defiance (breaks CC, 5s immunity), Revitalization Contract saves you at ≤10% HP.

## Common mistakes

- **Attacking from the front.** Shadowstrike, Ambush and Triniel's Dagger are built around back attacks.
- **Spreading points across every skill.** Whirlwind Slice, Infiltrate, Defiance and Flash Slice work fine at level 1.
- **Grinding instead of questing.** Story quests give far more XP.
- **Skipping Sealed Dungeons at 29–39.** They also give Skill Points.
